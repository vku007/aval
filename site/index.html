Case studies

Examples of typical enterprise engagements (anonymized where required)

High-throughput backend platform

Problem: reliability and performance bottlenecks under peak load.
Business impact: lower incident risk during peaks, faster user journeys, and predictable capacity planning.
Technical approach: profiling & p95 analysis, DB query/index tuning, connection pool & timeout hygiene, caching where safe, and baseline logs/metrics/tracing for fast incident triage.
Stack: Java, relational DB, AWS.

Data platform & storage strategy

Problem: inconsistent data models and slow queries across multiple systems.
Business impact: better data reliability for reporting/operations, reduced maintenance cost, and safer releases.
Technical approach: data modeling, indexing/partitioning strategy, query plan analysis, migration sequencing with rollback, and choosing the right store (Oracle/MySQL vs DynamoDB) per access pattern.
Stack: Oracle / MySQL / DynamoDB.

Big Data processing pipeline

Problem: slow batch jobs and fragile ETL pipelines.
Business impact: predictable batch windows, fewer failures, and improved cost/performance for analytics workloads.
Technical approach: Hive partitioning & file sizing, Spark tuning (shuffle, parallelism), incremental loads, idempotent job design, and adding operational metrics + alerting on SLA breaches.
Stack: Hive, Spark, AWS.
